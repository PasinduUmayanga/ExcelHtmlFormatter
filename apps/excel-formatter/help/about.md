# About

FormaFlow turns HTML tags in documents into real formatting. This version works with Excel workbooks.

## The name

FormaFlow was called **Excel HTML Formatter** before November 2026. Installing FormaFlow over it upgrades the app and keeps your tag styles. The settings folder keeps its earlier name, so your saved styles are found.

## Your data

FormaFlow works on this computer only and sends nothing over the internet. It stores three small files in its settings folder: your tag styles, your theme, and the recent activity list. Your workbooks are only read when you open them, and a new copy is written when you save.

## Reporting a problem

Include the version and platform shown on this page, what you did, and the message you saw. If you can, keep a copy of the workbook that caused it.
