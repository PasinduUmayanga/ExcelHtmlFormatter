# Getting started

Format your first workbook in five steps, and find your way around.

FormaFlow turns HTML tags inside Excel cells into real formatting. A cell containing `<b>my name</b> is <i>pasindu</i>` becomes **my name** is _pasindu_ in a new workbook. Your original file is never changed.

## Your first formatted file

1. On **Home**, **Excel** and **Format HTML tags** are already chosen: click **Continue**.
2. **Upload:** drop an `.xlsx` workbook or click **Choose file** (or **Load sample** to try the sample). Click **Continue**.
3. **Configure:** sheets that contain HTML are ticked for you. **Tag Styles** shows each tag that was found and how it will look; tags marked **No style** need a style, or their text is left plain. Click **Continue**.
4. **Preview:** FormaFlow checks that no text is lost, then shows the original and the result side by side. Click **Continue**.
5. **Export:** click **Save formatted file…** and choose a name. FormaFlow reads the new file back to check it, then shows what was saved, with **Open file** and **Show in folder**.

## What you need

- An Excel workbook in `.xlsx` format. Older `.xls` files and password-protected workbooks can't be opened: save them as `.xlsx` in Excel first.
- Cells whose text contains HTML tags, such as `<b>`, `<i>`, `<br>` or `<ul><li>`.

## Finding your way around

- **Home** is where you choose the kind of file and what to do with it, and see your latest files.
- The six steps are shown across the top of each workflow screen. They unlock when you start from Home (**Continue**, a template, or **Open** next to a recent file) and lock again after a save.
- The sidebar has **My Workspace** (your task in progress), **Recent Files**, **Favorites**, **Formatting Profiles** (your tag styles), **Templates**, **Settings** and **Help & Support**. **Automation** is planned and not available yet.
- **Ctrl+K** searches everything. **Theme** at the top switches between Light, Dark and System.
- **Help** at the top brings you back here. Use the search box on Help to find any topic.
